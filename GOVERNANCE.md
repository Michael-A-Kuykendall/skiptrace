# Skiptrace Governance

Skiptrace is maintained by its original author.

The project is open source and welcomes issues, discussion, and testing.
Major architectural or licensing decisions are made to preserve:

- openness of the scanner
- long-term sustainability of the project
- a stable report for people who put `--strict` in CI

Pull requests are restricted to approved maintainers. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
